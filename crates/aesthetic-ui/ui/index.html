How AESTHETIC Scores Your Shots (ELI5 Guide)

1,112 Shots / 199 Films
In Plain English (ELI5): Every candidate shot is measured across 79 optical, exposure, color, depth, staging, and motion numbers, then graded on a 0–100 curve built directly from real frames decoded out of Oscar, ASC, and BSC award-winning cinema (your Golden Baseline). It never guesses subjectively — every score and target traces back to real pixel math.

1. Your Overall Score (0–100)

Three measured pillars combine into one final score:

Video Clip: Overall = 45% Technical + 35% Creative Craft + 20% Temporal Motion
Single Still: Overall = 56.25% Technical + 43.75% Creative Craft (no temporal penalty)
  • Technical (45%): Exposure Dynamic Range (35%) + Highlight Roll-Off (35%) + Laplacian Optical Acutance (30%). Each is scored 0–100 on where it lands inside the Golden Baseline curve.
  • Creative Craft (35%): Gaze/Eyeline Poise & Lead Room (30%) + 3D Depth & Silhouette Separation (25%) + Color Harmony & CIE Gamut (25%) + Key-to-Fill & Catchlights (20%).
  • Temporal Motion (20%): Camera Stability (penalizes micro-jitter & judder 80%) + Motion Blur Coherence (20%).

2. How Golden Baseline Forms "Target" Numbers

AESTHETIC takes every measured shot & still frame in your Golden Baseline, sorts each metric from lowest to highest, and finds key percentiles:

  • p50 Target (The Median): The exact middle shot in the award-winning corpus (half of Master shots sit above it, half below). Think of it as the center of gravity of great cinema.
  • IQR (p25–p75 Sweet Spot): Where the middle 50% of award-winning cinema lives. Landing inside the IQR means your shot matches classic award latitude/contrast/acutance.
  • 0–100 Percentile Curve: min→0, p10→10, p25→25, p50→50, p75→75, p90→90, max→100. Whenever you add new films or stills, all targets recalibrate automatically!
  • Within-Style Targets: High-key comedies and low-key noir films shouldn't use the same target, so we also compute separate Within-Style p50 targets (e.g. Chiaroscuro Noir vs Prestige Naturalism).

3. Scene Scanner, Top N & Title Filtering

  • 100% Timeline Coverage: Pass 1 streams the whole video at proxy speed so every scene is discovered; Pass 2 scores every photographic scene at full resolution.
  • No Title Cards: Uniform borders & typography bands are flagged (NON_PHOTOGRAPHIC_OR_CREDITS) and stripped whenever real footage exists.
  • MMR Diversity & Top N: Picks the best shot from each unrepresented scene first (+10 pts priority) before adding second angles, sliced to your chosen Top N (or All).

4. Showreel Heads & Advisory Forensics

  • 6 Narrative Heads: Extra sub-scores in Contact Sheets rate Hero Potency, 3-Second Impact, Authorship, Storytelling, Cinematic Technique, and Memorability.
  • Non-Punitive Forensic Flags: Alerts like LOG_ENCODED_SIGNAL, ROLLING_SHUTTER_SKEW_RISK, and SUBTITLE_OR_LOWER_THIRD_BURNIN warn editors without blindly erasing intentional creative choices.
